You are commissioned by the Core Grid Architecture Commission to establish an optimal, fault-tolerant optical fiber network linking N distinct high-throughput computational hubs numbered sequentially from 1 to N.

There are M bidirectional high-bandwidth transmission conduits available for installation. The i-th optical conduit links Hub ui and Hub vi with an infrastructure routing cost of wi petajoules per terabit.

Due to quantum photonic switching hardware limitations at gateway switches, the degree of Hub 1 (the primary hyper-computing core) in the selected minimum spanning backbone must not exceed K connections. You must synthesize a connected topology spanning every hub while minimizing the sum of transit weight costs under this degree constraint. If no valid topology can be formed, return -1.
